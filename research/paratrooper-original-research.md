# Original Paratrooper Game Research\n\n## Aircraft Mechanics\n\nThe original Paratrooper game was created in 1982 for DOS/PC by Greg Kuperberg and published by Orion Software. After researching available resources, here's what we know about the aircraft in the game:\n\n### Wave Progression\n- The game starts with helicopters only\n- After reaching a certain score threshold or defeating a number of helicopters, jets/planes appear\n- The game becomes progressively more difficult with faster enemies and more frequent spawns\n\n### Aircraft Types\n- **Jets/Planes**: Faster than helicopters, appeared to be simple fighter jets\n- Unlike helicopters, jets didn't drop paratroopers\n- Instead, they dropped bombs directly aimed at the player's turret\n\n### Flight Patterns\n- Jets flew in straight horizontal lines similar to helicopters\n- They generally flew at a higher altitude than helicopters\n- They moved faster across the screen than helicopters\n\n### Bombing Mechanics\n- Jets would drop bombs when positioned roughly above the player's turret\n- Bombs fell in a straight vertical line or slight arc\n- If bombs hit the turret, it would be destroyed (game over)\n- Bombs could be shot down with the turret\n\n### Scoring\n- Jets were worth more points than helicopters when shot down\n- Shooting down bombs also awarded points\n\n### Visual Characteristics\n- Jets were typically drawn as simple shapes resembling fighter aircraft\n- They had no rotating parts (unlike helicopter rotors)\n- Bombs were small objects that fell toward the ground\n\n### Implementation Notes\n- Jets should be implemented similar to helicopters but with different graphics and behavior\n- No need for trooper-dropping capability\n- Need to implement a bombing mechanism when over the turret\n- Bombs should have their own collision detection\n
